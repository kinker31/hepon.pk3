# hepon.pk3: It's like wepon.pk3, but for Heretic!
Anything majorly reworked, as well as any new mechanics, will be explained in this file, starting with...

## The Sparkly Orb System
Enabled by default, monsters killed will drop a set amount of sparkly mana orbs that fade away after some time. Collecting enough of them will allow you to modify how your current weapon functions.

Each one can be selected using the User1 to User4 keybinds (the hepon options menu lets you define them), and pressing/holding the Zoom key allows you to spend them to modify your weapon.

Discover and use them all!

## Item Subclasses
(Can only be found, among other things, if Extra Items are on)
### Quartz Flask
Potent Brews heal you more if you're closer to death, but heal less if you're healthy.

Sludgy Catylists damage you upfront, but give you idle regeneration powers.

### Timebomb of the Ancients
Normal Timebombs are now thrown, and deal more damage.

Timebomb Mines stop almost immediately, and take a long time to explode, but can be used for ambush tactics.

Boucing Timebombs need to bounce a couple of times before reaching their full potential. Great for trickshots!

Cluster Timebombs throw a whole bunch of them at once, dealing major damage to anything in front of you.

### Tome of Power
Cursed Tomes damage you to 10HP, but the powerup lasts much longer.

Super Times last much shorter, but doubles your damage output and blocks 50% of damage.

### Misc.
Fires more projectiles per item, and you can carry more of them.

Slipspheres let you move faster, but friction is reduced.

Quad Damage rings do what they imply, but only for a short time.

## Reworked Weaponry
While most of your stuff should function similar to vanilla, anything new, or majorly reworked weapons, will be listed here.

* __**Staff:**__ Does more damage if you're moving faster, Altfire rewards combo hits.
* __**Gauntlets:**__ Leeches health be default, altfire leeches your health in exhange for a free ranged attack.
* __**Gold Wand:**__ Altfire turns your wand into a tiny little railgun.
* __**Crossbow:**__ Altfire allows you to overstuff the bow, firing more arrows at the cost of higher ammo consumption.
* __**Dragon's Claw:**__ Fires much more slower by default, but can be fed Wand Crystals to speed it up for a bit.
* __**Hellstaff:**__ Altfire shoots out orbitals that block a set amount of damage for a set amount of time, but constantly drains ammo as it does so.
* __**Phoenix Rod:**__ Altfire trades in some ammo in exchange for making you stronger, as well as shooting out mini-birbmissiles when damaged, at the cost of taking more damage.
* __**Firemace:**__ Best fired in outdoor areas, with a lot of monsters, and preferrably not for too long. Hope your computer can handle the tomed version!
